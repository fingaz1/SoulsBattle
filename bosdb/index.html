The warrior who wanted the throne himself

As the Battle of Souls tournament rages on, one summoned warrior begins to see the tournament for what it is. He turns his fellow warriors against the Immortal who summoned him, overpowers him, and takes his master's power to summon combatants for his own. Now he enters the tournament himself, aiming to rule the immortal realm as its king.

You and your fellow Immortals discover the plot and set your rivalries aside. This time you're not fighting each other — you're fighting to stop him before he wins.

ONE OF SIX, RANDOMIZED EACH GAME

Every playthrough, a different Legendary Warrior

At the start of each game, one of six Legendary Warriors is drawn to lead the enemy pyramid — each with its own set of effects. The threat you're facing is never quite the same fight twice.

BATTLEFIELDS & SOUL POINTS

Where you fight, and what it costs

Battlefield cards play a major role in the fight — each one triggers a different effect depending on which rank activates it. Legendary Warriors and Warlords set off one effect; Warriors and Elite Warriors set off another. Soul Power comes in three currency colors, and every purchase from the supply line draws on it.

NO TWO GAMES ALIKE

A different table every time you sit down

Fighters and support cards are shuffled fresh each game — some land in the Legendary Warrior's pyramid, others in your supply line. What you're able to recruit, and what you're up against, changes every playthrough.
