The immortal plane doesn't run out of dead.

You are one of the Immortals, a race as old as Earth itself, who have watched every war the world has ever fought. Now the Battle of Souls has begun — a tournament to crown a new king of the immortal realm. Summon history's warriors back from the earth, Samurai and Vikings among them, and send them to battle once more. Drain your rival immortal's soul power, and the throne is yours.

A few faces from the field

Every fighter carries their own stats and abilities — warlords who turn the tide of a match, warriors built for the front line, and equipment that changes the fight outright.

How a turn unfolds

01

Deploy your fighters

Bring Warlords and Warriors onto the field, each carrying their own ATK and DEF. Some abilities only trigger the moment they're deployed.

02

Equip and support

Equipment and effect cards can swing a fight mid-battle — reducing an opponent's attack, protecting your line, or setting up your next move.

03

Claim the souls

Win the exchange and take control of the field. Hold more souls than your opponent when the fighting ends, and the realm is yours.

Two histories, one battlefield

Build your side of the war from two very different traditions of combat.

Samurai

Disciplined warlords who bend the field to their will — commanding tribute, seizing cards from an opponent's hand, and turning honor into raw battlefield control.

Vikings

Warriors built for momentum and resourcefulness, trading their own strength on the field for the cards and battlefields they need to win.

The realm keeps growing

Samurai and Vikings are just the opening battle. More warrior traditions from across history are already on the way.

Spartan

In production now — the next tradition of warriors set to enter the realm.

Aztec
